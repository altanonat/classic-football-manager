# Classic Football Manager — web version

A **fan-made, CM 01/02-inspired football management game** that runs entirely
in your browser — no download, no account. Take over a club, pick tactics,
work the transfer market, and play through seasons minute by minute.

**What's inside:**
- 64 leagues with current squads (as of October 2026): 17 top flights,
  13 second divisions with promotion and relegation, plus U21 and U18
  academy leagues
- Full seasons with fixtures, tables, domestic cups, continental cups and
  international tournaments
- Live match view, attribute search, staff, training and board confidence

**Just a test project.** This is an unofficial fan game made for fun — it is
free, non-commercial, and not affiliated with any club, league or publisher.
Player ratings are my own estimates, not official data. I built it because I
love this kind of game and thought someone else might enjoy playing it too.

Feedback and bug reports are welcome — enjoy! ⚽
